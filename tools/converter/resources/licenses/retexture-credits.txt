==========

Installation:

Copy TH06e_ST into your EoSD folder. When prompted, overwrite everything.

Now, select between Visible and non-visible hitbox. Copy the th06e_CM and TH06e_TL from your choice and put them into your EoSD folder. When prompted, overwrite everything.

Finally, select your preffered sound pack. Copy th06e_in and put it into your EoSD folder. When prompted, overwrite everything. Even if you're using the original sound effect, please copy the th06e_ST.dat from the 'Original' folder, as this contains the new fonts.

-

NOTE: The Retexture pack only works properly with an English patched version of the game. If you install this with the Japanese version, in-game dialogue will appear broken. If anyone's interested in helping create a version that works correctly on the Japanese version, feel free to visit the current thread on: http://www.pooshlmer.com/wakaba/

The 'Patch Making and Extraction Tools' may also be of interest: http://touhou.wikia.com/wiki/Random_Things

The patch will also bump up the minimum requirements for the game. However, this will only become a problem if you are using a very low spec computer.
